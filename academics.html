Our Academic Programs

We are committed to quality education and the development of every learner.

Nursery Education

We support early childhood learning through play, creativity, and foundational skills.

Baby Class

Supporting young learners as they begin their educational journey.

Middle Class

Helping learners develop early learning skills, creativity, and confidence.

Top Class

Preparing learners with foundational skills for primary education.

Primary Education

We help learners to develop literacy, numeracy, critical thinking, and practical skills.

Primary 1

Building first foundation of reading and writing

Primary 2

We develop what they have passed through previous class including: writting , reading and simple operations through classroom learning.

Primary 3

Strengthening literacy, numeracy, and learning skills.

Primary 4

We encourage learners to develop understanding and critical thinking through starting learning simple science.

Primary 5

We help learners to understand fully what they have been studied in previous stages to prepare for the National examination in P6.

Academics Programs

The school offers the Rwandans Competence-Based Curriculum (CBC) from preschool to primary levels, with emphasis:

Literacy development

Involves development of writting and reading activities , speaking , and to understand information.

Numeracy

Involves development of mathematical operations.

Science and technology

Involves development of science and technology practices.

Christian religious values

Our school teaches learners about christian values and wishes of God on their life.
