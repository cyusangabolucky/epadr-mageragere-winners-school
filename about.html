About Our School

Welcome to EPADR Mageragere Winners School, a private school committed to provide quality education and helping learners to develop their talents. Teaching learners christian values and preparing them as future leaders.

Background

EPADR MAgeragere winners school is a christian-based educational institution located in Mageragere Sector, Nyarugenge District, Kigali city. Founded in 2017 by Pastor Kigenza Emmanuel, the school was established to provide quality, value-driven education to children in the community. From it's beginnings with a small number of learners and limited facilities, the school has steadly expanded in both enrollment and infrastructure. Guided by Christian values including love for God, Love for others , integrity, And compassion, the school is committed to nurturing learners who are academically competent,morally grounded, and socially responsible.

Our mission

To ensure that our learners achieve their full potential in terms of revelant knowledge, skills and appropriate attitude and values that prepare them to be well integrated into society and be kind of future citizens the country needs. With these learners will gain appropriate skills and knowledge which will help them to grow spritual and mental.

Our vision

Our vision is Community where all children feel loved, Respected and encrouraged to develop to their fullest-potential
